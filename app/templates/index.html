03 / MACHINE LEARNING BENCHMARKS

Model Evaluation & Comparison

Six classification algorithms evaluated on an unseen 20% chronological holdout test set ($n=1,172$) with 5-fold TimeSeriesSplit cross-validation on training data.

Engineering Discovery (500-Ohm Threshold): Decision Tree and Random Forest achieve 100% test accuracy because the hardware controller operates on an exact deterministic rule: Soil Moisture Resistance ≥ 500 Ω → Pump ON. Distance-based models (KNN, SVM, Logistic Regression) exhibit minor boundary smoothing around 500 Ω.

05 / WATER QUANTITY REGRESSION

Irrigation Volume Estimation

STATUS: PENDING DATA COLLECTION

Volumetric Water Flow Prediction is Currently Inactive

The primary objective of automated precision irrigation is not merely binary pump switching, but exact volumetric dosing (liters or m³ per irrigation cycle).

Why is Regression Disabled?

The current dataset (project_dataset.csv) contains actuator binary status (DC Water pump Status) but lacks quantitative flow meter or applied water volume measurements.

Scientific & Ethical Guardrail

In adherence to rigorous empirical principles, no synthetic water volume target has been fabricated, and pump status is not falsely modeled as a continuous water volume quantity.

Future Roadmap

Integration of in-line pulse water flow meters (e.g., YF-S201) will enable high-precision regression models (Gradient Boosting / ElasticNet) to forecast exact liters required per irrigation event.
